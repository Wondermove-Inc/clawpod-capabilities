# Safety and approval

Require explicit review for real-person face editing, public figures, political persuasion, trademark-sensitive assets, copyrighted characters, adult content, minors, high-impact decisions, and external publication.

Block non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual content involving minors, deceptive impersonation, credential imagery, and unlawful use. Provider safety success never replaces human rights, consent, or publication review.

Bind paid approval to the exact prompt and input digests, provider, model, operation, options, output count, dimensions, destination, pricing snapshot, maximum USD, secret binding, and expiry. Any change invalidates approval.

After timeout, disconnect, malformed response, or 5xx following possible acceptance, mark billing as ambiguous. Reconcile the original request or job. Never automatically resubmit. Retry 429 only when provider evidence proves rejection before billing.
