# Common organizational templates

Use the shared header for every family, then the matching body. Replace bracketed prompts with facts. Record work in the task service designated by the applicable `WORKFLOW.md`; write a neutral `[system-of-record reference]`, never a fixed product name.

## Shared header

- Title and family:
- Context and intended outcome:
- Priority or severity:
- Responsible owner:
- Recipient and, if different, decision authority:
- Needed-by time or next-update expectation:
- Current state:
- Evidence and links:
- Explicit requested action:
- System-of-record reference:

## 1. Delegation or task request

Expected result; in/out of scope; constraints; acceptance criteria; deadline; authority and dependencies; acknowledgement method.

## 2. Peer-help request

Blocked goal; attempts; observations; expertise or action needed; interaction mode; time box; exit condition.

## 3. Upward status or decision

Conclusion first; status and impact; options and trade-offs; recommendation; one decision question; decision deadline; people to inform.

## 4. Blocker or escalation

Blocked result; known cause and unknowns; impact and time sensitivity; attempts; authority or decision needed; alternatives; next escalation condition.

## 5. Handoff or shift change

State and last-verified time; completed/in-progress/pending work; hypotheses with confidence; next actions; risks; role/contact map; explicit acceptance.

## 6. Review or approval request

Purpose and change summary; related work; scope; risks; verification results; review focus; approval criteria; decision authority and deadline.

## 7. Incident update

Status/severity; impact; confirmed facts; actions underway; assigned roles; brief timeline; unknowns; next update time or condition.

## 8. Completion or closure

Outcome; evidence per acceptance criterion; verifier and time; residual impact/risk; follow-ups with owner and deadline; tracking reference; recipient acceptance or closure authority.

## 9. No-response follow-up

Original request and time; still-needed action; impact; new response deadline; alternate contact or escalation path; default action if no response.
