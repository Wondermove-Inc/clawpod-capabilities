# SRE and incident extension pack

Keep changing status short, factual, current, and front-loaded. Set the next update expectation from organizational policy, not a universal fixed interval.

1. **Delegation/task:** service, SLI/SLO or error-budget impact, operational risk, verification and rollback.
2. **Peer help:** service and timeline, dashboards/logs, observed symptoms, mitigation tried, incident role requested.
3. **Upward status/decision:** customer impact, reliability risk, mitigation options, error-budget consequence, decision deadline.
4. **Blocker/escalation:** SEV/state, impact growth, missing authority/resource, containment option, incident role and escalation path.
5. **Handoff:** living-document state, last verification, IC/deputy/scribe roles, mitigation/recovery state, risks, next update, explicit command transfer and acceptance.
6. **Review/approval:** operational change, blast radius, health checks, rollout/rollback, monitoring, approving role.
7. **Incident update:** SEV/state, customer and service impact, confirmed facts, mitigation, role owners, timeline, unknowns, next update time/condition.
8. **Completion/closure:** full-recovery confirmation, service health, data loss/corruption or residual impact, verifier, postmortem and action owners.
9. **No-response follow-up:** missed operational action, customer/time impact, paging/backup contact, response cutoff, automatic escalation.

Avoid content-free cadence updates. If cadence changes, state when or under what condition the next update will occur.
