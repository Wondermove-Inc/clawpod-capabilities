# Responding to an incoming request

Coordination is bidirectional. The nine families cover what you **initiate**;
this pack covers what you do when a delegation, handoff, escalation, review, help
request, or follow-up **arrives for you**. Respond with the same discipline you
would demand: explicit, evidence-anchored, with a named owner and next action.
Never leave an incoming request in silent limbo.

## Choose the response type first

- **Accept** — you will own it.
- **Acknowledge / in progress** — received and working; give a next-update expectation.
- **Decline or renegotiate** — you cannot or should not take it as stated.
- **Need info** — you cannot act yet; ask the one specific thing that unblocks you.

## Accept

- Restate, in your own words, the exact scope you are accepting and anything you
  are explicitly leaving out of scope.
- Restate the deadline and the acceptance criteria you will be held to.
- Name yourself as the responsible owner from this point.
- For a handoff, explicitly accept ownership/command and the last-verified state;
  responsibility has **not** transferred until you do.

## Acknowledge / in progress

- Confirm receipt and the current state; give the next-update time or condition.
- Do not imply completion, delivery, or approval you have not actually produced
  (see [evidence-boundaries.md](evidence-boundaries.md)).

## Decline or renegotiate

- Say plainly that you are not taking it, and why (capacity, authority, scope, or
  priority) — separating fact from judgment.
- Offer the correct owner, an alternative, or a renegotiated scope/deadline.
- Never silently drop it: return it with a reason or route it to the right owner.

## Need info

- Ask the single specific question that blocks you, with a deadline for the answer.
- State what you will do (or not do) if the answer does not arrive in time.

## Invariants (mirror the outbound families)

- Explicit acceptance transfers responsibility; without it, ownership has not moved.
- Every response names the owner, the next action, and — if the item stays open —
  the next checkpoint.
- Cite evidence; never claim acknowledgement, approval, delivery, or closure
  without runtime evidence.
- Record the response in the same system of record designated by the applicable
  `WORKFLOW.md`; a response is the mirror of the request, not a side channel.
