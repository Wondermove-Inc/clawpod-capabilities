# Errors and recovery

Handle INVALID_ARGUMENT, NOT_FOUND, CONFLICT, PREREQUISITE_MISSING, UPSTREAM_CONTRACT_INVALID, AUTH_REQUIRED, AUTH_INVALID, APPROVAL_REQUIRED, BUDGET_EXCEEDED, GATE_PENDING, PATH_VIOLATION, DEPENDENCY_FAILURE, PROVIDER_FAILURE, RATE_LIMITED, TIMEOUT, CANCELLED, CANCEL_FAILED, PARTIAL_FAILURE, SCHEMA_VIOLATION, DIGEST_MISMATCH, and INTERNAL_ERROR. Retry only declared transient failures. Preserve partial outputs, report unknown effects, and resume only from a revalidated checkpoint.
